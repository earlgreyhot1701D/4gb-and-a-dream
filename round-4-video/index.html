§ Round 04 · 4 GB and a Dream

The tour

Every round, in 47 seconds. No sound.

How it was made: Claude drove a browser in its cloud workspace with a script, recorded it, and encoded the MP4 with ffmpeg. Nothing was recorded or edited on the Chromebook. It only plays the result.

What's honest about it: it was recorded from the repo files, which are the same files GitHub Pages serves. Necesito uses a demo number, and the tap stops at the button: a browser can't film Messages opening. The 3D frame-rate meter is hidden, because it would show the recording machine, not the Chromebook.

The recording script is in the repo: scripts/record_tour.py.
