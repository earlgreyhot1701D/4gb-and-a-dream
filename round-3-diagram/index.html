The bootstrapping path

Round 3 · How a 4 GB Chromebook ships a live app. Only two steps happen on the laptop.

On the laptop In Claude's cloud workspace On GitHub

Press Next step, or let it play.

  1. 💻
    On the laptopDescribe itYou type what you want into a Claude chat in Chrome. Nothing to install.
    Wi-Fi down
  2. 💬
    In the cloudDecide itClaude asks one question at a time. You pick. The answers become the spec.
  3. 🛠️
    In the cloudBuild itClaude writes the code, runs it, and takes screenshots to check its own work.
  4. 📦
    On GitHubPush itYou approve access to one repo. Claude commits and pushes from the chat.
  5. 🌐
    On GitHubShip itYou turn on GitHub Pages: Settings, Pages, main, Save. About a minute later, it's live.
  6. 👀
    On the laptopSee itYou open the link. This is the only other step your laptop does.

↺ The feedback loop: see it, ask for a change in the chat, and go around again.
